## Our own placeholder art (CC0)

Generated by `tools/make_pixel_portraits.py`: the HD pixel portrait parts (`assets/portraits/`).
Generated by `tools/make_placeholder_art.py`: flags (`assets/flags/`),
goods icons (`assets/icons/goods/`).
Generated by `tools/make_pixel_art.py`: pixel window frames and the top bar strip (`assets/ui/pixel/`), top bar pictograms (`assets/icons/hud/`),
side-view battle ships (`assets/ships_side/`), the map's top-down ships (`assets/ships/`) and battle backdrop props (`assets/battle/`).
Generated by `tools/make_map_trees.py`: the world map's trees (`assets/map/trees.png`).
Generated by `tools/make_scenes.py`: the painted scenes for ports, events and endings (`assets/scenes/`).
Shaders in `assets/map/`, `assets/battle/` and `assets/ui/` are our own.
Replace any file with real art at the same path (or change the path in `data/`), and add a row above for it.

No AI-generated images are used as game assets.
