## Our own placeholder art (CC0)

Generated by `tools/make_placeholder_art.py`: ship layers (`assets/ships/`), flags (`assets/flags/`), portrait parts (`assets/portraits/`),
goods icons (`assets/icons/goods/`), the paper panel (`assets/ui/panel.png`). Shaders in `assets/map/` are our own.
Replace any file with real art at the same path (or change the path in `data/`), and add a row above for it.

No AI-generated images are used as game assets.
